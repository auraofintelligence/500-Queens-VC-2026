Council of Queens.

Twenty-four creative AI mentors connect enterprise knowledge, imagination, identity and belonging.

GenAI concept artwork

A council you can learn with

The AI Queens mentorship system, also called the Council of Aura or Council of 24, gives different kinds of expertise a memorable face. Twelve Civilisation Pillar Queens organise the work people might build. Twelve Cultural Anchor Queens bring identity and cultural perspectives into the learning design.

Participants would develop the characters and their meaning through a mutable mythos: a shared creative world that can change as people contribute. The purpose is practical. A founder can explore a decision from several angles, rehearse a difficult conversation and prepare a clearer budget, experiment or presentation.

The twelve Cultural Anchor Queens

Rainbow Serpent Queen, Dragon Queen, Lotus Queen, Olive Queen, Crescent Queen, Coral Queen, Clover Queen, Flame Queen, Phoenix Queen, Sun Queen, Pearl Queen and Prism Queen form the cultural and identity strand of the original design.

Together, the proposed strands span First Nations, East and South Asian, Mediterranean, Middle Eastern and Central Asian, Pacific, Celtic, African, Southeast Asian, Anglo-colonial, mixed-heritage and inclusive perspectives. These are creative AI characters. Cultural knowledge, interpretation and permission remain with the people involved. Ready S.E.T. Co-op's cultural intelligence approach connects a contribution to its source, purpose and agreed uses.

Rehearse a decision, then test it

Imagine exploring a new water service with River, Forge and Shield. River helps frame the need; Forge asks what can be built and measured; Shield helps prepare the questions about agreements, intellectual property and funding. The participant makes the decision, with appropriate human expertise and the people affected by the work.

A session would begin with an enterprise brief and approved sources. The participant explores alternatives, prepares a result and takes it to a human mentor for review. Recording the decision and what happened afterwards gives the next session better evidence.

Build the mentorship service

Establishment means assembling 24 character briefs, connecting approved source libraries and testing realistic business scenarios with prospective participants. Hosting, maintenance and human mentoring need a costed delivery plan.

Measure accuracy, usefulness, cost and access. Corrections and well-sourced examples can improve the service over time. The Council is currently a mentorship design to develop; the site introduces its characters and method without claiming that a live AI mentoring service is operating.
