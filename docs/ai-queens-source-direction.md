# AI Queens: corrected source direction

Recorded 24 September 2026 after Luke corrected the artwork interpretation.

## The distinction

The 24 AI Queens are deliberately chosen mythic mentor archetypes. They are distinct from the real women participating in 500 Queens Venture Capital. Do not use demographic casting for community photographs as the art direction for the AI Council.

Luke's latest direction is authoritative: archetypal goddesses who have mastered life, ageless adults in their prime, embodying vitality, intelligence and an idealised best self. Their physiques range from sporty and athletic to heavily voluptuous. The AI portraits are not to use overweight body designs. They must have elegant, widely varied clothing, individual faces, hair, colours, silhouettes, settings and presence. This is fictional character art direction, not a health assessment of real women.

The broader community imagery remains a separate brief representing real women of different ages, sizes, appearances, places and life circumstances.

## What went wrong in the replacement prompts

The generated character briefs invented ages and substituted unrelated appearances for the original cultural identities. They repeatedly requested plum or purple corporate clothing and the same studio/boardroom treatment. Broad exclusions removed the mythic character. Reusing that template across the Council produced uniformity rather than the intended distinct archetypes.

## Requirements for the next artwork pass

- Preserve all 24 names, the two councils, each original cultural identity and each domain.
- Keep the mythic, goddess-like presence and mastered intelligence central to every character.
- Use ageless adult presentation in physical prime; distinguish athletic, lean, curvaceous and heavily voluptuous forms without making a single silhouette the default.
- Use the original individual colours as character cues. The website's royal palette is not a uniform for the Queens.
- Develop varied elegant clothing suited to each character. Do not default everyone to suits, gowns, modest high necklines or identical poses.
- Give every Queen a recognisable face, hairstyle, stance, environment and visual expression of her domain.
- Keep identity and original source attribution visible in the profiles, rather than replacing the cultures with generic belonging language.
- Keep hero copy outside the artwork so it does not hide the characters.
- Review the complete Council together for repetition as well as reviewing each image individually.

The source review preceded the replacement artwork. Following the explicit generation request, the 24 September 2026 correction uses individual prompts in `data/queens-art-direction.json`, restores the original identities to the profiles and keeps all image compositions free of text overlays.
