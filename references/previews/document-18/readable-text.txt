Section  1.1:  From  Inquiry  to  Legislation:  A  Deliberate  and  Consultative  
Path

The

 The  Bill  is  a  direct  and  logical  consequence  of  the  token

tailored  to  address  this  specific  harm.
20
 Its  core  logic  is  to  regulate  the  activity  of  providing  a

This  is  achieved  through  a  significant  amendment  to  the  Corporations  Act  2001 ,  which

1
 The  creation  of  these  new  product

The

 ●  Digital  Asset  Platform  (DAP):  Defined  as  a  facility  where  an  operator  holds  digital

20  
●  Tokenised  Custody  Platform  (TCP):  Defined  as  a  facility  where  an  operator  holds  an  
underlying
 
asset
 
(which
 
can
 
be

Under  the  proposed  legislation,  any  entity  providing  these  DAP  or  TCP  facilities,  or  providing

 This  triggers  a  comprehensive

A  key  feature  of  the  Bill's  architecture  is  its  deliberate  technology-neutral  language.  The  draft
