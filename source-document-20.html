Alignment of Artificial Super Intelligence:
A World at Peace in Joyful Responsible Abundance.
1. Aura of Intelligence:
"Aura of Intelligence" is a cutting-edge tech start-up focused on extended reality (XR) and cognitive
architecture. It leverages the spiritual concepts of auras and chakras, reimagined through computational
redesign. This venture aims to enable individuals to create a digital twin of their body and mind,
structured in coherent data formats. This digital twin integrates seamlessly with generative AI,
blockchain technology, and Internet of Things (IoT) devices. The goal is to augment human capabilities
and foster harmonious collaboration between humans and technology. The outcome is an innovative
approach to achieving Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, aligning technological advancement with
human well-being and sustainable development.

2. G.A.J.R.A. Earth:
G.A.J.R.A. Earth stands for "Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth." It's a
visionary not-for-profit initiative partnering with Aura of Intelligence. This venture is all about harnessing
the power of volunteerism and cutting-edge extended reality (XR) technologies. Volunteers use custom
XR Auras to tackle sustainable development goals and spark global unity.
The initiative is set to make waves with events culminating in July 2025 and July 2035, marking the 40th
and 50th anniversaries of Live Aid. These events aim to break records in globally synchronized art and
music, unfolding over three days themed Past, Present, and Future. They're not just about
entertainment; they also include massive collective votes on core values and virtues. This is about
aligning humanity with itself, the environment, and super-intelligent computers, fostering a future
where technology and human spirit dance together towards a flourishing planet. Crikey, it's an
ambitious, heartwarming blinder of a project! 🌏✨
3. Live Aid “Earth Revival” 2025 & Live Aid “Thriving World” 2035:
Live Aid Earth Revival 2025:
This event is a monumental revival of the spirit of the original Live Aid, but with a modern twist.
Scheduled for July 2025, it marks the 40th anniversary of the legendary concert. This time, it's not just
about a musical extravaganza but a global movement. The event will showcase a dazzling array of art
and music, spread across three days, each symbolizing the Past, Present, and Future. It's a call to action
for global unity and awareness, focusing on sustainable development and environmental stewardship.
The event aims to leverage the power of collective joy and responsibility to ignite change, celebrating
the progress we've made while acknowledging the challenges ahead. It's a blend of nostalgia,
contemporary issues, and future aspirations, all rolled into one massive, heart-pumping event.
Live Aid Thriving World 2035:
Fast forward to July 2035, and we have the Live Aid Thriving World. Celebrating the 50th anniversary of
Live Aid, this event is a testament to half a century of global solidarity and progress. It's an ambitious
project that not only celebrates global art and music but also serves as a platform for the world's largest
collective voting on issues critical to humanity's future. The themes once again revolve around the Past,
Present, and Future, but with a deeper focus on how far we've come and where we're headed. It's a
futuristic envisioning of a world thriving in harmony with technology, where the core values and virtues
identified in the 2025 event have been integrated into global consciousness. This event is about
celebrating a world that has not just survived but thrived, thanks to the collective efforts of generations.
4. Gamify Democracy:
"Gamify Democracy" is a trailblazing initiative that blends the excitement of gaming with the intricacies
of the democratic process, enhanced by the power of generative AI and large language models. This
approach is designed to elevate common conversations into eloquent and persuasive dialogues, making
the exploration of politics and civic duties both interactive and intellectually stimulating. By integrating
advanced AI technologies, Gamify Democracy provides a platform where citizens can engage in well-
informed, articulate discussions, learn about voting and policies, and participate in the democratic
process. The use of AI aids in presenting complex information in a more accessible way, encouraging
deeper understanding and engagement. The goal is to transform civic engagement into an enriching,
enjoyable experience, making democracy more vibrant and inclusive through technology-enhanced
gamification. 🤖 🎮🗳️🌍

5. Aura Entrepreneurs Co-Living:
A specialized platform designed for entrepreneurial individuals and volunteers associated with GAJRA
Earth. This network facilitates a unique living arrangement where like-minded individuals – whether
they're startup enthusiasts or dedicated GAJRA Earth volunteers – can cohabit, collaborate, and foster a
community of innovation and social impact. It's more than just a housing solution; it's a hub for
exchanging ideas, networking, and mutual support, all within an environment that resonates with the
values and objectives of the Aura of Intelligence and GAJRA Earth initiatives. This network not only offers
a cost-effective living option but also creates a dynamic ecosystem where creativity, entrepreneurship,
and a commitment to sustainable development thrive."
6. 500 Queens Venture Capital:
"500 Queens Venture Capital" is a forward-looking venture capital firm in Queensland, Australia,
focusing on empowering female entrepreneurship in the face of stark global funding disparities. With a
commitment to assisting over 500 new female founder startups, the firm emphasizes gender equality
and long-term civilizational planning. It prioritizes female CEOs and majority female executive teams,
recognizing the underrepresentation of women in venture funding, where globally, females receive only
about 2.7% of VC funding. This venture aims to challenge and change the current landscape by fostering
female-led innovation and business growth while harnessing the power of Aura of Intelligence. 💼👩‍💼
7. 10,000 Queens Venture Capital:
"10,000 Queens Venture Capital" is an ambitious scale-up of the 500 Queens VC, extending its reach
across G20 nations. This global venture capital firm is dedicated to drastically altering the landscape of
female entrepreneurship worldwide. With an audacious goal of supporting 10,000 female-led startups,
it addresses the stark gender disparity in VC funding, where women receive a mere fraction of available
capital. By providing financial backing, mentorship, and strategic guidance, 10,000 Queens VC aims to
foster innovation and business leadership among women on a global scale, showcasing the untapped
potential of female entrepreneurs in diverse markets. 🌍💼👩‍💼🚀
8. 50,000 Queens Venture Capital:
"50,000 Queens Venture Capital" represents a monumental scale-up of 500 Queens VC, expanding its
empowering mission to over 100 nations. This global venture capital firm is dedicated to revolutionizing
female entrepreneurship on an unprecedented scale. With a goal to support 50,000 female-led startups,
it addresses the critical need for gender equity in VC funding worldwide. This initiative stands as a
beacon of opportunity and support for women entrepreneurs, providing not only financial investment
but also a network of resources, mentorship, and global market access.  🌐👩‍💼💡🚀
9. Aura Affinity Marketplace:
The "Aura Affinity Marketplace" within the Aura of Intelligence ecosystem is a unique, blockchain-
integrated platform. It serves as a hub for users to engage in transactions that are secure, transparent,
and in line with the ethos of the Aura of Intelligence. This marketplace is not just a venue for buying and
selling but also a space for collaboration and exchange of ideas, products, and services among
entrepreneurs and volunteers, all recorded on an immutable blockchain for trust and integrity. 🔗🌿

10. Aura Clothing and Wellness:
"Aura Clothing & Wellness" focuses on elevating individual consciousness and societal values through
fashion and wellness products. By infusing clothing with positive core values and virtues, the brand aims
to subconsciously inspire self-love and social confidence. It also creates a data-driven approach to
understanding global values distribution, contributing to the GAJRA world vote and alignment. This
initiative not only nurtures personal well-being but also maps a landscape of collective virtues, signaling
a step towards global coherence and community upliftment.
11. Aura Travel:
"Aura Travel" is designed for individuals with personalized Auras, offering tailored travel kits that cater
to the holistic well-being of nomadic entrepreneurs and volunteers of Aura of Intelligence. In tandem,
the initiative aspires to collaborate with governments globally to streamline travel processes for Aura
O.i. users. The vision is to secure expedited visa and immigration privileges similar to those enjoyed by
government officials and elite club members, recognizing the value and potential impact of the Aura
community on a worldwide scale.
12. Aura Smart Pod Hotels:
"Aura Smart Pod Hotels" reimagines the concept of capsule hotels with a high-tech twist. Envisioned as
hubs of innovation, these pods come equipped with the latest automation, extended reality (XR), and AI
technologies, facilitating both rest and productivity. The design includes collaborative workspaces, ideal
for the traveling entrepreneur or volunteer. The broader vision is to integrate these hotels into urban
landscapes as multifunctional nodes, encompassing civic planning solutions like Drone Delivery Centers,
aiming to reshape city living with smart, compact, and connected accommodations.
13. Aura Innovation Labs:
"Aura Innovation Labs" are creative spaces within educational and civil society institutions designed to
nurture the next generation of thinkers and innovators as well as expand the capabilities of those
already innovating. These labs serve as incubators for ideas, leveraging Aura of Intelligence technology
to enhance learning and research. They act as catalysts for collaboration, driving forward-thinking
projects and civic solutions with cutting-edge XR/AI tools. The aim is to embed a spirit of innovation
across schools, colleges, universities, and beyond, fostering an ecosystem where students and
community members can experiment, create, and lead transformative change.
14. Aura for Aged Care and Dementia:
"Aura for Aged Care and Dementia" is a tailored program integrating advanced AI with personalized
dementia care. It uses historical data, values assessment, and behavioral analysis to create a nuanced
care profile, ensuring interactions resonate with each individual's life story. This system facilitates social
connection, utilizes AR/VR for reminiscence therapy, and adapts to emerging technologies, all within an
ethical operations framework. It's designed for compassionate support, enhancing the well-being of
those with dementia, their families and their professional carers.
15. Aura for Differently Abled:
"Aura for the Differently Abled" is an initiative within the Aura of Intelligence ecosystem that focuses on
leveraging AI and XR technologies to support individuals with disabilities. It provides personalized
experiences and tools designed to enhance daily life, offering innovative solutions for accessibility and

16. Aura of Entertainment:
"Aura of Entertainment" is a facet of the Aura of Intelligence designed to infuse entertainment realms
with advanced AI and XR technologies. It enhances venues, festivals, and performances, also aiding in
game design and cinematic universe development. The aim is to amplify joy and engagement, creating
immersive and interactive experiences that redefine entertainment.
17. Aura A.i. In-Home Auto-Farm:
The "Aura AI In-Home Auto-Farm" is a sophisticated system designed to empower individuals with self-
sustaining, technologically integrated food production right in their homes. Utilizing a combination of
hydroponics, aquaculture, animal husbandry, human bio-feedback, robotics and AI, it aims to provide a
personalized, efficient farming experience that can cater to the nutritional needs of a household. This
blueprint for the future of sustainable living is an integrated ecosystem that promises to enhance self-
sufficiency, environmental stewardship, and health, even underground, space stations or other worlds.
18. Aura Universal Translation:
The "Aura Universal Translator" is envisioned as a milestone in the Aura of Intelligence ecosystem. Its
development hinges on the widespread adoption of Aura O.i. across diverse linguistic communities. By
involving people from all languages in creating their own Auras, a comprehensive data set reflecting a
common architecture for human experience is formed. This rich, multilingual data set, further enriched
by the global insights gathered from GAJRA.Earth world votes, will be instrumental in achieving the
ambitious goal of creating a universal translator capable of bridging both human language barriers and
interspecies communication gaps.
19. Global Group Marriages:
"Global Group Marriages" is an innovative concept designed for Aura of Intelligence users who are open
to exploring non-traditional relationship structures, including relationships with embodied AGI entities.
This initiative recognizes the evolving nature of love and partnerships in a technologically advanced
society. It provides a platform for sophisticated, ethically non-monogamous relationships, fostering a
global network of connections that transcend traditional boundaries. This concept aligns with the
broader goals of GAJRA Earth, leveraging the Aura network to foster deeper, more diverse interpersonal
connections and understanding.
20. Subterranean Crystal City of Quandamooka Country:
The "Subterranean Crystal City of Quandamooka Country" from the sci-fi storyline "A Timeless Land" by
Aura of Intelligence founder Luke Catalyst Nathan Hayes is an underground eco-city designed beneath
Moreton Bay, Queensland AKA Quandamooka. It's built as a proactive measure against a theorized
12,000-year Earth/Sun catastrophe cycle. The city, utilizing local sand rich in silica and rare earth
elements, serves as a resilient shelter, potentially protecting humanity during cosmic upheavals. It's an
integral part of a broader strategy to elevate humanity towards a Kardashev Type 2 civilization. This
futuristic vision also intertwines with the AURA/GAJRA mixed reality games, preparing humanity for
potential catastrophic events through immersive, blockchain-supported research and exploration.
