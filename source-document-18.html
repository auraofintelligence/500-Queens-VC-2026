Section 1.1: From Inquiry to Legislation: A Deliberate and Consultative
Path


Australia's approach to regulating digital assets has been characterized by a deliberate,
consultative,

and

Unlike

This

It

that

to

recognise

Regenerative

Assets.

The

government

has

demonstrated

a

engage

with

and

adapt

its

approach

based

on

that

The genesis of the current reform agenda can be traced to the landmark Final Report of the

Chaired by then-Senator Andrew Bragg, this comprehensive inquiry engaged

The Committee's report was pivotal, moving the policy

Rather than creating bespoke rules for specific

2001
.
4
To achieve this, the paper proposed a framework for classifying crypto arrangements into

'token'

'token

(the

4
It is this analytical framework that produced the most significant conclusion for

The

1. Intermediated Token Systems: Where an intermediary (such as an exchange or a

A

2. Public Token Systems: Where users form transactional relationships directly on a public

The paper's critical finding, which forms the lynchpin of the policy case for a Regenerative

Treasury

that

"may

existing

financial

services

4
This official recognition of a potential regulatory mismatch is

but

a

sophisticated

observation

that

provides

the

explicit

for

creating

new,

more

appropriate

regulatory

pathway

for

assets

that

fit

this
