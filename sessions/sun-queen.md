## Open a leadership opportunity more fairly

An enterprise is recruiting a project lead through familiar personal networks.

1. Describe the capability the work actually requires.
2. Examine how the opportunity is advertised and whose experience counts.
3. Ask potential applicants about practical barriers and revise the process.
4. Review the outcome and the authority given to the appointed person.

## A useful result

A clearer role brief and a participation review.

## Human review

Responsible decision makers and affected participants examine the proposed changes.

## Questions

- Which requirement is essential to the work?
- Who never hears about this opportunity?
- What advantage are we treating as neutral?
- If this institution were designed for equal agency today, what would women change first?
