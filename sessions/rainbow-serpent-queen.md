## Prepare before proposing a place-based project

A founder wants to develop a local environmental experience that includes community knowledge.

1. Write what the venture proposes and which people and places it involves.
2. Separate public material from knowledge someone would need to choose to contribute.
3. Prepare an introduction and a budget for people's time.
4. Let the relevant people shape whether and how the conversation proceeds.

## A useful result

A clear discussion brief and a record of questions for the appropriate people.

## Human review

Relevant First Nations people determine participation, authority and agreed uses; the founder follows their actual decisions.

## Questions

- Whose place and whose knowledge are involved?
- Who has asked for this work?
- What control will contributors retain?
- What do the women involved say they want from this work, and what power will they have to shape it?
