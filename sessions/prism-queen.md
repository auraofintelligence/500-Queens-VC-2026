## Make the next workshop usable

A network wants a broader range of women to participate in a venture workshop.

1. Ask prospective participants what would help them take part.
2. Review the invitation, venue, timing, materials and online route.
3. Provide clear choices and a way to request an arrangement privately.
4. Gather feedback about actual participation and make the next revision.

## A useful result

A workshop access plan and a short review of what worked.

## Human review

Participants define their needs and assess their experience; appropriate specialists review technical access.

## Questions

- Who can use this as it currently works?
- What are we asking people to disclose unnecessarily?
- Which choice would remove a barrier?
- What would you redesign if women with your body, needs and way of thinking helped set the starting rules?
