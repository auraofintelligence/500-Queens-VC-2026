## A launch people can understand

Your first service trial is ready, but the announcement reads like a technical report.

1. Name the people invited and the useful result they could experience.
2. Turn the service into a short explanation and one concrete example.
3. Choose an accurate image, an accessible format and a clear next action.
4. Test the announcement with a small audience and revise confusing parts.

## A useful result

An honest launch message, a channel plan and a feedback sheet.

## Human review

Someone from the intended audience checks the meaning, while the delivery team confirms every promise.

## Questions

- What will a stranger understand in the first ten seconds?
- Which claim can you demonstrate?
- What do you want someone to do next?
- How do you want to be seen, and which expectations about women's appearance do you want to leave behind?
