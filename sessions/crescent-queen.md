## Choose a partnership that fits

A founder receives a promising partnership offer but is unsure how it fits her enterprise's stated values.

1. State the values that matter in the founder's own words.
2. Identify the actual decisions or activities affected by the offer.
3. Prepare open questions about expectations and possible arrangements.
4. Compare the answers with the founder's priorities and seek relevant advice.

## A useful result

A values-and-operations comparison and a partner discussion agenda.

## Human review

The founder and actual participants determine the commitments; relevant advisers review specialist matters.

## Questions

- What does this value require us to do?
- Which difference can be accommodated through a practical arrangement?
- What have we assumed instead of asking?
- What do you need to participate fully while remaining the author of your own choices?
