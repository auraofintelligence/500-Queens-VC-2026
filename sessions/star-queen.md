## Give a space idea an Earth-side experiment

A team proposes a distributed observation system for better environmental or space-weather understanding.

1. Specify the question and the existing sources of evidence.
2. Identify the missing measurement and alternatives for obtaining it.
3. Design a small reproducible analysis or ground-based demonstration.
4. List what the result would establish and what would remain untested.

## A useful result

A research decision brief with assumptions, competing approaches and a measurable next experiment.

## Human review

Relevant researchers and intended users examine the method and interpretation.

## Questions

- What evidence would distinguish these explanations?
- Which dependency could stop the whole plan?
- What remains useful if the larger vision takes longer?
- Which future would give women more real choices over knowledge, resources and time?
