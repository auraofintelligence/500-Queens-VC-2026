## A new leader in an established business

A woman is considering taking responsibility for a family or community business with a strong history.

1. Listen to customers, workers and the current owner about what matters.
2. Map authority, key relationships and the knowledge that needs transferring.
3. Choose a first improvement with explicit responsibility and a review date.
4. Prepare the commercial and governance questions for appropriate advisers.

## A useful result

A transition discussion brief and a first leadership assignment.

## Human review

The actual owners, workers and prospective leader review the proposed transition.

## Questions

- What would people miss if this business disappeared?
- Which tradition still serves its purpose?
- Where does the new leader need real authority?
- What would the next generation of women change if they could redesign this enterprise now?
