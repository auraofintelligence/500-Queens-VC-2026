## A next chapter with a practical bridge

A woman returning to enterprise wants to use her experience in a different industry.

1. List useful capabilities and the work she wants to move towards.
2. Speak with people doing that work about actual needs.
3. Design a small paid service, placement or demonstration where possible.
4. Review the result alongside time, income and further learning needs.

## A useful result

A transition map and one concrete project proposal.

## Human review

The founder and relevant practitioners review whether the proposed bridge is realistic.

## Questions

- What are you taking forward?
- What are you ready to stop carrying?
- Which small project could create new evidence of your capability?
- What support would make your next chapter a real choice rather than another demand?
