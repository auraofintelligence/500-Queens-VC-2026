## Make one scene carry the vision

A founder wants to explain a future community system through a short film or interactive experience.

1. Choose the person whose day reveals the idea.
2. Outline a moment of difficulty, a meaningful change and an honest unresolved question.
3. Create a storyboard or small playable scene.
4. Show it to a relevant audience and record what they understood and felt.

## A useful result

A creative treatment, one prototype scene and an audience response record.

## Human review

Creators, contributors and intended viewers review the experience and its claims.

## Questions

- Whose perspective leads the story?
- What can the audience discover through experience?
- Which contribution needs agreement before publication?
- Which women rarely get to imagine themselves as the person making the future?
