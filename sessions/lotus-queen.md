## Make room for a serious ambition

A founder wants to start a venture and needs to discuss changing time and responsibilities with people close to her.

1. Write the ambition and the reasons it matters in the founder's own words.
2. Map current responsibilities without assuming who should carry them.
3. Compare a few possible work and support arrangements.
4. Rehearse an honest conversation and identify the next practical agreement.

## A useful result

A personal priorities brief and a proposed working arrangement.

## Human review

The founder decides what to share; people involved negotiate actual commitments together.

## Questions

- Which parts of this life do you want to keep?
- What do you want permission from yourself to pursue?
- Which responsibility needs a new arrangement?
- Which environment would let your ambition, relationships and independence flourish together?
