## From notebook to first test

You want to improve everyday food access but have three different business ideas and no clear starting point.

1. Describe one person's difficult moment, including what happens now.
2. Write three possible responses and the assumption behind each.
3. Choose the smallest demonstration that could challenge the most important assumption.
4. Prepare five open questions and an invitation to a willing participant.

## A useful result

A one-page idea brief, a rough demonstration and a dated learning task.

## Human review

A prospective user and a human mentor check whether the test explores a real need.

## Questions

- What have you noticed that other people take for granted?
- Which person could show us how this works today?
- What would make you change your mind?
- What kind of environment would let you try an idea without having to look like someone else's idea of a founder?
