## A founder story that feels like your own

A founder is preparing a profile and feels pushed to choose one simple identity narrative.

1. Write what she wants the audience to understand about her work.
2. Choose relevant experiences in her own language.
3. Remove details included only to meet someone else's expectations.
4. Test whether the introduction supports a useful conversation.

## A useful result

A founder introduction and a clear account of what remains personal.

## Human review

The founder retains final choice over her identity, story and publication.

## Questions

- Which words feel accurate to you?
- What connection can you see from your particular experience?
- What are you tired of being asked to explain?
- Which parts of your environment make room for who you choose to be?
