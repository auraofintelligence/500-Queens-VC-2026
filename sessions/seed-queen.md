## A small harvest with a clear purpose

A team wants to supply fresh produce from a shared growing facility.

1. Speak with intended buyers about quantities, timing, quality and existing suppliers.
2. Map a small production cycle including growing, packing and delivery.
3. Record water, energy, labour, losses and the resources needed to repeat it.
4. Review the result with a grower and customers before proposing expansion.

## A useful result

A pilot production plan and a simple record of cost, yield and customer response.

## Human review

Experienced growers, intended customers and relevant food specialists examine the plan.

## Questions

- Who wants this harvest and when?
- Where does the hardest work fall?
- What happens in a poor season?
- What would rural women need to lead this system, including paid time, reliable services and a life beyond the work?
