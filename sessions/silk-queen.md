## One market, one credible relationship

A small manufacturer has interest from an overseas distributor and wants to prepare the first serious discussion.

1. Clarify the product, customer and volume being discussed.
2. Research existing alternatives and prepare questions for local participants.
3. Map supply, support and proposed partner responsibilities.
4. Prepare an introduction pack and a list of terms for specialist review.

## A useful result

A partner briefing, delivery map and focused follow-up agenda.

## Human review

Experienced local market participants and relevant trade advisers review the assumptions.

## Questions

- Why would this customer choose your offer?
- What can your prospective partner tell you that a market report cannot?
- Can you support the product after delivery?
- What conditions would let you participate internationally without sacrificing the life you want at home?
