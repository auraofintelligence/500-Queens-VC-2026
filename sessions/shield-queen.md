## Prepare for a first partnership agreement

A larger organisation offers space and introductions in exchange for an interest in your developing enterprise.

1. Write down the actual offer and separate confirmed terms from conversation.
2. List each party's contributions and proposed decision rights.
3. Mark missing information, possible conflicts and questions about ending the arrangement.
4. Prepare a comparison brief and questions for an independent legal or financial adviser.

## A useful result

A negotiation worksheet and a concise adviser briefing pack.

## Human review

The founder and appropriately qualified advisers review the actual documents before any commitment.

## Questions

- What does each party receive?
- Who owns the work if the relationship ends?
- Which promise has no written definition yet?
- What control, income and ownership would make this opportunity fair for you?
