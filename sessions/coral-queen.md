## A shared service with a local future

Two communities are considering a shared food or energy demonstration.

1. Let each group describe the need and the work already underway.
2. Compare the resources and responsibilities each is willing to contribute.
3. Choose a limited shared result and local decision process.
4. Plan ownership, maintenance and a review after the demonstration.

## A useful result

A reciprocal project brief with named responsibilities and a continuity plan.

## Human review

Participating communities and relevant operators decide whether the arrangement serves their priorities.

## Questions

- Who defined the need?
- What does each group receive from the exchange?
- Who can keep the service working?
- What do women in this community want the service to make possible in their own lives?
