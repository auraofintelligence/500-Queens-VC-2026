## Decide how the shared budget is used

A growing network must choose between several worthwhile projects and members are unsure who has authority.

1. List the actual decisions and the money available.
2. Identify the authorised decision makers and affected participants.
3. Agree the criteria, information, conflicts process and consultation method.
4. Record the decision, reasons and a date to examine its consequences.

## A useful result

A decision map and a repeatable meeting and record template.

## Human review

Members and the organisation's responsible human officers review the proposed process.

## Questions

- Who can make this decision?
- Who experiences its consequences?
- What happens when a decision maker has a conflict?
- If women designed this institution now, what would change about authority, time, care and participation?
