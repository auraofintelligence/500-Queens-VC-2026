## A creative night with a working model

A founder wants to open a community arts space but has not tested the programme or operating effort.

1. Choose one activity with a clear audience and purpose.
2. Map the venue, equipment, access, paid work and booking needs.
3. Run a small session and ask participants what they would return for.
4. Compare income, cost, time and the experience before planning more.

## A useful result

A programme brief and a record of participation and operating effort.

## Human review

Participants, workers and the venue operator review the experience and practical arrangements.

## Questions

- What makes this place worth coming back to?
- Who might feel like an outsider?
- Whose work is currently invisible?
- What would make women from different places and stages of life feel able to shape this space?
