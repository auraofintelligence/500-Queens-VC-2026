## From impressive sketch to working part

A founder has a concept for a modular structure and needs to decide what to make first.

1. Choose one function that must work for the whole concept to succeed.
2. Describe loads, dimensions, interfaces and relevant operating conditions.
3. Ask suitable makers to review the brief and propose a limited prototype.
4. Measure the result, record failures and revise the next build.

## A useful result

A prototype specification, a test record and a revised production question.

## Human review

Qualified engineers or tradespeople review safety-critical requirements and build methods.

## Questions

- What must this part actually do?
- Which dimension or property matters most?
- How will it be repaired?
- Would this workshop's tools, clothing and decision-making arrangements fit the women who want to work here?
