## A better supplier conversation

A founder is discussing a prototype with an overseas manufacturer and emails are producing conflicting expectations.

1. Describe the product and unresolved questions without idiom or vague promises.
2. Ask how the partner handles specifications, changes and approval.
3. Confirm the proposed sequence in a shared written summary.
4. Have technical and language specialists review consequential details.

## A useful result

A shared conversation agenda and an explicit list of confirmed and open points.

## Human review

The actual partners check the record; relevant specialists examine technical and contractual details.

## Questions

- What have they actually agreed to?
- Which assumption comes from the person, and which from us?
- Who needs to review the next step?
- What would make the working relationship fair and useful to the actual women on both sides?
