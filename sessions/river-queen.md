## A water service with a measurable baseline

A founder wants to offer local water monitoring but has not chosen the customer or decision it would support.

1. Ask prospective users which water decisions are currently difficult.
2. Identify existing records and the remaining information gap.
3. Prepare a limited monitoring plan with a baseline and interpretation support.
4. Compare the usefulness and cost of the result with existing alternatives.

## A useful result

A monitoring service brief, a baseline plan and a customer review question.

## Human review

Local participants and relevant water specialists review the sampling and interpretation.

## Questions

- Where does this water come from and go next?
- Which decision needs a better measurement?
- Who will keep the equipment working?
- Which women experience this place differently, and what would they need to shape its water decisions?
